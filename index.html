About

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at Tel Aviv University, jointly supervised by Dr. Inbar Seroussi and Dr. Dmitry Batenkov.

My research focuses on mathematical models of neural networks in high-dimensional settings. Previously, I completed my MSc at Tel Aviv University, where I studied physics-informed neural networks for atmospheric radiative transfer.

Research interests

  • Learning theory, transformers, and in-context learning
  • Learning collective dynamics and probabilistic forecasting
  • Physics-informed neural networks
